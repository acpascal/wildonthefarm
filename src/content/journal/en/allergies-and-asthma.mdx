_Sometimes, nature hides surprises in plain sight. My story begins 15 years ago, in the lush Talamanca mountain range of Panama._

---

## From Allergies to an Unexpected Discovery

Fifteen years ago, I developed my **organic farm** in the Talamanca mountains, right on the border with Costa Rica.
One day, while walking through the surrounding jungle, I was **severely stung by a plant**.

I had suffered from *severe allergies* for years, making daily life miserable. Assuming it might be a plant from the nettle family, I took a risk and **consumed its seeds** — something I would never suggest anyone do with an unidentified wild plant.

A few months later, while traveling with my children and admiring massive blooming trees, I suddenly realized something: **my allergies were gone** — and they have never returned since. I can't prove the plant caused it. I can only say what I noticed.

---

## A Friend's Experience with Asthma

Back on the farm, while continuing my project, I received a visit from a friend. We went on a **jungle tour to observe Quetzals**, the iconic birds of our region.
The high-altitude, humid air triggered an **asthma attack** for him, and he used his inhaler, as he always does.

Once he'd recovered, I told him about my own experience. Some time later, out of curiosity and entirely on his own judgment, he chose to try a small amount of the same plant's seeds.

In the years since — 14 now — he tells me he hasn't had another asthma attack. I can't explain why, and I'm not a doctor. I'm only recounting what he's told me.

---

## What I Still Don't Know

Since then, I've heard from a handful of other people who tried the plant on their own and reported similar relief from allergies or asthma. I take these accounts seriously, but they remain anecdotes, not evidence.

What I do know:

- I have never had the plant formally identified or studied.
- It's unrelated to the nettle family, despite how it first stung me.
- It could behave very differently in someone else's body, in a different dose, or under different circumstances.

That's precisely why I'm not naming it, offering it, or recommending it here.

---

## Hitting a Wall with Institutions

Hoping to get it properly studied, I asked a lawyer to help me reach out to laboratories and research organizations.
There was no interest, no reply — nothing.

Years passed, and I've come to accept that a home-grown, unstudied plant with an unverified backstory isn't the kind of thing institutions are set up to chase down, however genuine the story is to me.

This left me with a mix of **frustration and disappointment**.
I wasn't asking for money — just for the chance to have it looked into properly.
