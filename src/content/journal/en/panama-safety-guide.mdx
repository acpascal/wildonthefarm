## High-Risk Areas To Avoid

The vast majority of Panama is straightforward for travelers, but a small number of specific areas warrant real caution:

- **The Darién Province:** the jungle border with Colombia, known for trafficking and armed groups. All travel here is strictly discouraged.
- **The city of Colón:** high rates of urban crime and gang activity. Avoid walking around outside of secured port areas.
- **Specific Panama City neighborhoods:** El Chorrillo, Calidonia, Curundú, and San Miguelito require absolute caution day and night.
- **The Mosquito Gulf:** a remote Caribbean coastal area heavily affected by drug-trafficking routes.

## Safety in Chiriquí and Boquete

The mountainous Chiriquí province enjoys a very peaceful reputation. Boquete is one of the safest towns in Panama, highly popular with expats and retirees — active local neighborhood watches and a visible police presence keep crime exceptionally low.

The main hazards up here are environmental rather than criminal: slippery trails during the rainy season, or getting lost in sudden mountain fog. ## Key Crime Statistics

- **Global Peace Index:** Panama consistently ranks as one of the top three safest nations in Central America.
- **Crime Index:** scores around 45–47 out of 100, considered moderate and comparable to major global tourist destinations.
- **Targeted crime:** over 85% of recorded offenses involve non-violent property theft rather than violent crimes against individuals.
